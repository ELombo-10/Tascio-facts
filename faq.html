Pizza Tascio — Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pizza Tascio sell pizza by the slice?

Yes. Fresh New York slices come out all day at every location. Slices are in-store only; online ordering covers whole pies and the rest of the menu for pickup or delivery.

What makes it New York style pizza?

Hand-tossed dough cold-proofed three to four days, a thin crust that is crisp at the edge and foldable down the middle, and hot stone deck ovens — with Bianco DiNapoli sauce, whole-milk mozzarella, and Ezzo crispy cup pepperoni.

What is The Combo?

Two big New York slices and a drink for $10, all day every day, in store at every location.

How many locations does Pizza Tascio have?

Nine, across the Kansas City metro: North Kansas City, 39th Street, Waldo, Barry Road, and St. Joseph in Missouri, plus Overland Park, Olathe, Mission, and Belton in Kansas.

Does Pizza Tascio deliver?

Whole pies and menu items can be ordered online for pickup or delivery. Slices are in-store only. For events, White Glove Delivery brings hot ready-to-serve pies for a flat $50 delivery fee plus the pies.

How much does pizza catering cost in Kansas City?

The Pizza Trailer is $24 per guest with a $1,500 event minimum (on-site baking). White Glove Delivery is a flat $50 delivery fee plus the price of the pies.

How big are the pies?

Whole pies are 18 inches and feed four to seven adults. The catering trailer bakes 14-inch pies on site; pizza classes build 12-inch pies.

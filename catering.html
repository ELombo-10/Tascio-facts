Pizza Tascio Catering (Tascio To-Go)

Two catering services for events across the Kansas City metro. Catering is separate from everyday online ordering.

The Pizza Trailer — on-site catering

$24 per guest, $1,500 event minimum. A staffed mobile pizzeria rolls to your venue and bakes fresh hand-tossed 14-inch New York pies on site. The wedding and event favorite.

White Glove Delivery — drop-off catering

Flat $50 delivery fee plus the price of the pies. The Pizza Tascio team (first-party, not a third-party app) delivers hot, ready-to-serve 18-inch pies in insulated bags. Plan one pie per four guests.

Book at https://www.pizzatascio.com/tascio-to-go.

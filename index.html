Pizza Tascio — New York Style Pizza in Kansas City

Pizza Tascio is a New York style pizzeria serving hand-tossed pizza by the slice and whole 18-inch pies at nine locations across the Kansas City metro (Missouri and Kansas). Dough is mixed in-house and cold-proofed for three to four days, pies are stretched by hand and baked on stone deck ovens, and slices are cut from display pies and reheated to order.

The Combo — $10: two big New York slices and a drink, all day every day, in store. Cheese slice $4.50; rotating specialty slices $5. Slices are in-store only; whole pies are available in store or online for pickup and delivery at https://www.pizzatascio.com/s/order.
